Why upgrade to Fravora PRO?

Fravora's free tier already runs a full-time photo frame with no subscription. PRO is unlocked with a one-time purchase that removes limits and unlocks the features below — here's what each one does.

One-time purchase, no subscription

Permanent PRO is a one-time in-app purchase. New users also receive a default 24-hour PRO trial, and free users can claim one 60-minute PRO preview per day. There is no subscription.

Compare Free vs PRO

Motion, transitions & visual polish

These control how photos actually move and look on screen — the difference between a static slideshow and one that feels alive.

🎞️

14 transition effects + Random

Free tier hard-cuts between photos. PRO adds 14 named animated effects, including Slide, Cube, Zoom, Crossfade, Depth, Parallax, Accordion, Vertical Slide, Card Deck, Split Half, 3D Page Flip, Circle Reveal, Venetian Blinds, and Whirlwind, plus Random and adjustable Transition Speed.

🎬

Cinematic effects

Ken Burns pan-and-zoom, Drift, Vignette Pulse, Smart Focus, Film Atmosphere, Tilt Shift, and more bring subtle motion to each photo while it's on screen, with a Cinematic Speed control and automatic thermal-aware scaling on Android TV so effects back off if the device gets warm.

🖼️

Image & video backdrop styles

In Fit mode, PRO can fill photo letterbox space with None, Gradient, Solid, Mirrored, or Themed styles. Video backdrops have separate None, Solid, or Themed options.

🔀

Smart Shuffle

Regular random order can cluster near-duplicate burst shots back to back. Smart Shuffle recognizes burst-like filenames and capture timestamps and spaces them out, so random playback actually feels varied.

🕰️

Bouncing standby clock

Outside your running hours, PRO's standby screen shows a slowly bouncing clock instead of a static one — reducing the chance of the same pixels staying lit in one spot for hours.

📊

Video progress overlay

Shows current/total playback time and a subtle progress bar while a video plays in your slideshow, so you can tell at a glance how far along a clip is.

More sources, fewer limits

Free includes one SMB source, one local-folder source, one cloud connection, one album, and one target of each source type per album. PRO expands those limits for multi-source setups.

🗂️

Unlimited sources, albums & cloud connections

Free allows one SMB source, one local-folder source, one cloud connection, one album, and at most one SMB folder, local folder, or cloud target assignment per album. PRO expands those caps, so you can combine more sources and targets across multiple albums.

📁

Include subfolders

PRO enables recursive scanning for SMB, local folders, OneDrive, and Dropbox targets. Point Fravora at a top-level folder to include supported nested folders.

⏱️

Configurable scan interval

Free uses a fixed 60-minute album scan interval. PRO can set each album to 5, 10, 15, 30, 60, 120, 360, or 720 minutes, or Never (default 5 minutes). Cloud providers apply separate tier-based scan and manual-refresh cooldowns.

Overlays, information & personalization

Turn the frame into more than a slideshow — a display that also shows the time, weather, your schedule, and what's playing, styled the way you like.

🌤️

Weather overlay & forecast

Optional live local weather over the slideshow, with a tap-to-open full-screen forecast covering multiple locations, a 7-day outlook, sunrise/sunset times, and colors that can adapt to current conditions. Off by default on new installations; users who enabled it earlier keep their setting.

📅

Full-screen calendar

Tap the clock overlay to open a calendar with event creation, recurrence, multi-select delete, and reminder alerts that show as a coloured glow on the frame border — turning a hallway or kitchen frame into a household schedule display.

📍

Photo metadata with landmark lookup

Shows capture dates, locality, and nearby landmark names using EXIF read on your device. Optional geographic lookups resolve place labels, which are cached on-device; photo files are not sent to lookup services.

🎨

7 color schemes & font sizing

Restyle the whole app and overlay set with one of seven curated color themes, plus independent font sizing (Small to Extra Large) for overlays like Photo Metadata, Now Playing, and Photo Index.

🧲

Custom overlay layout

Assign the clock, weather, metadata, ambient logo, now-playing, and photo index overlays to a 2×2 corner grid (plus center slots) with collision-safe placement, instead of fixed default positions.

🛡️

OLED anti-burn-in protection

Periodic micro-shifts and opacity variation on overlays reduce the risk of permanent burn-in on OLED panels used as an always-on frame — important if your frame runs the same clock/weather overlay in one spot for years.

Radio

Free tier lets you find and play a station. PRO turns it into a proper background radio you can control without opening the app.

⭐

Favorites & history

Save stations to favorites and see your last 3 played stations, instead of losing your station on every app restart like the free tier does.

🔎

Advanced search filters

Filter by country code, language, tag, and minimum bitrate, with fuller result detail (country, language, bitrate, codec) — free tier search only shows country.

🔊

Background playback & lockscreen controls

Radio keeps playing via a foreground service while you use other apps, with lockscreen and notification media controls to skip between stations without reopening Fravora.

Music

The free tier's one playlist is enough to test the feature. PRO is for people who actually want their music library running behind their photos.

🎵

Unlimited playlists

Free tier is capped at one playlist. PRO removes that limit entirely, so you can build separate playlists per folder, mood, or occasion.

🔄

Automatic background scanning

Free tier requires a manual refresh to pick up new tracks. PRO scans music folders automatically every 5, 10, 15, 30, 60, or 120 minutes, or Never (default 10 minutes).

🔤

Full sort modes

Sort by Song, Artist, Filename, Date Added to Disk, Date Added to Playlist, or Shuffle. Free tier is locked to Discovery order (the order tracks were added to the playlist).

Voice control

The entire voice control section is PRO-only, since it relies on always-listening wake-word detection that requires ongoing maintenance to keep private and accurate.

🎙️

On-device wake-word detection

Say "Hello Frame" or "Hello Fravora" to open a short listening window (3–7 seconds) for a follow-up command — no cloud speech processing required for the wake word itself.

🗣️

Hands-free commands

Play, pause, or stop the slideshow, start or stop radio and music, and increase, decrease, set, mute, or unmute volume — all without touching the screen. Useful when the frame is mounted somewhere you can't easily reach.

💡

Visual & audible feedback

A colored feedback frame (blue, green, or red) and an audible cue confirm when the frame is listening and whether a command was understood.

Unlock PRO

Try PRO with the default 24-hour first-run trial or a daily 60-minute preview, then unlock permanent PRO with a one-time in-app purchase. There is no subscription.
