Download Fravora and start your smart photo frame

Fravora is available on Google Play and the Amazon Appstore for Android tablets and display devices. Start with first-launch onboarding, local folders or Google Photos, then expand to NAS/SMB, radio, weather overlays, and voice controls later.

Compatibility overview

Fravora is built for Android devices used as dedicated displays. It works especially well on tablets, docked devices, wall-mounted screens, and repurposed Fire tablets.

  • Android tablet and display device support
  • Google Photos, local, NAS/SMB, and supported cloud sources
  • One-time 1-hour PRO trial for new users on first launch
  • Offline slideshow support with local and NAS paths
  • No subscription required for core photo frame use

Before you install

For the best experience, keep your device plugged in, disable aggressive battery optimization for long sessions, and choose a stable mount or stand location.

Quick start after install

  • Complete first-launch onboarding (sources + first album)
  • Review or adjust your album selection for playback
  • Set slideshow interval and display mode
  • Optional: set NAS reconnect retry interval for SMB outage recovery
  • Optionally enable weather, radio, overlays, and voice control

What’s free and what’s PRO

Fravora’s free tier covers everything you need to run a full-time photo frame with no time limit and no ads during playback.

  • Free: local folders, NAS/SMB source, Google Photos and other cloud sources with a baseline cloud connection, slideshow with standard orders and hard-cut transitions, scheduling, clock overlay, hidden photos management, radio search and playback, one music playlist
  • PRO: unlimited sources and albums, include-subfolders recursion, transition effects (10 styles), cinematic motion (Ken Burns, Drift, Vignette Pulse), weather overlay and full-screen forecast, photo metadata overlay with landmark lookup, voice control, radio favorites and background playback, unlimited music playlists with auto-scanning and full sort modes, Smart Shuffle, image backdrop styles, calendar with events and reminders, OLED anti-burn-in effect, color schemes, and more

PRO is a one-time in-app purchase. No subscription. New users also get a free 1-hour PRO trial on first launch, and optional rewarded ads can unlock temporary PRO access at any time.
