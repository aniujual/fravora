Build a High-Performance Music Frame with NAS & Android

Published August 20, 2026 · 7 min read

While many users turn their Android tablets into digital photo frames, a growing number of enthusiasts are using them as dedicated NAS Music Streamers. Fravora can read music directly from a Synology, QNAP, or Windows network share with high-performance playback and folder-based playlist management.

Why stream music from your NAS?

Cloud streaming services are convenient, but they often lack support for high-fidelity local files (FLAC, ALAC) or deep metadata customization. By using a NAS photo frame setup for audio, you keep your files private, avoid subscriptions, and leverage the storage you already own.

Key v1.1.2 Music Features for NAS Users

  • Folder-based playlists: Add whole folders or individual files and keep playback organized by folder structure.
  • Manual refresh: Rescan on demand and review Added/Removed/Re-linked counts.
  • Smart Re-linking: If your NAS IP changes or files move, Fravora restores access or re-links tracks by relative path or identity token.
  • Automatic background scanning: PRO can rescan on a configurable interval.

Step-by-step NAS Music Setup

  • Connect your Synology or QNAP NAS in Settings → Sources using the SMB provider.
  • Go to Settings → Audio → Music and create a new playlist.
  • Use the Add Folder action and select your music root on the network share.
  • Use Refresh Now to verify Added/Removed/Re-linked counts.
  • Set the Scan Interval (PRO) so Fravora automatically discovers new music added to your NAS.

This setup allows your Android device to act as a high-quality music hub that plays alongside your photo slideshow, complete with background playback and lockscreen controls.

Deduplication and Library Integrity

Fravora's deduplication system ensures that your library stays clean. It uses an identity token (normalized filename + size + date + title + artist) to prevent duplicate tracks, even if they exist in multiple folders on your NAS. On the Free tier, this is always active to keep things simple; PRO users can toggle it for specific playlist needs.
