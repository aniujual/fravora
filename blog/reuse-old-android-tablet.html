Best ways to reuse an old Android tablet

Updated June 27, 2026 · 6 min read

Old tablets are still useful if you give them a single, always-on job. One of the most practical options is turning that device into a digital photo frame display for your home with Google Photos, NAS, or offline local playback.

Top reuse ideas that still make sense in 2026

  • Kitchen display for recipes and family photos
  • Desk dashboard with calendar and weather
  • Bedside smart clock and photo slideshow
  • Living-room wall frame for daily memories

Why a photo frame setup is usually the best choice

Compared with ad-hoc tasks, a dedicated frame setup puts minimal strain on old hardware and provides visible value every day. You can run local content or a Google Photos slideshow, avoid forced ads, and keep power connected for reliable playback.

Quick conversion checklist

  • Factory reset if the tablet is cluttered and slow
  • Install Fravora and complete first-launch onboarding to choose Google Photos, local, or NAS photo sources and create your first album
  • Set slideshow interval and active hours
  • Enable only the overlays you want
  • Use a stand or wall mount and keep charging continuous

This gives you a smart photo frame for Android that works without buying dedicated frame hardware or paying for another subscription.
