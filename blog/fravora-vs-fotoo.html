Fotoo alternative for Android: Fravora vs Fotoo compared

Updated August 10, 2026 · 7 min read

Fotoo is one of the most downloaded digital photo frame apps on Android — over a million installs and a solid reputation for supporting NAS, cloud sources, and weather overlays. If you are evaluating photo frame apps and Fotoo came up in your search, this comparison covers the practical differences: licensing model, feature depth, and long-term cost.

Is Fravora a good Fotoo alternative?

If you are looking for a Fotoo alternative because of the per-device licensing cost, the lack of a free tier, or missing features like Immich support, voice control, or radio — Fravora covers all of those gaps. It runs on any Android tablet, has a free tier with no time limit, and PRO is a single purchase not locked to one device. The NAS and cloud source support overlaps significantly with Fotoo, so switching does not mean losing the features you already rely on.

Licensing: free tier vs per-device purchase

This is the most practically significant difference. Fotoo's paid license is per-device — if you want to run it on two tablets, you pay twice. This is a real friction point for users who want to set up a second frame or replace a device, and it shows up in Fotoo's own reviews.

Fravora's free tier covers the core photo frame experience indefinitely: slideshow playback, local and NAS sources, scheduling, clock overlay, and basic radio. PRO is a one-time purchase and is not locked to a specific device. If you are setting up one frame, the cost difference may be small. If you are setting up multiple devices or planning to replace your tablet eventually, the licensing model matters more.

Where the apps overlap

Both apps support NAS/SMB network shares, Google Photos, OneDrive, Dropbox, weather overlays, EXIF location display, and Android TV. If your requirements are primarily cloud sources plus NAS plus weather, both apps solve the problem. The question then becomes licensing cost, feature depth beyond those basics, and which app's interface and configuration you prefer.

Where Fravora goes further

Beyond the overlap, Fravora adds features Fotoo does not have: voice control with wake-word detection, internet radio with a now-playing overlay, local music playlists, an in-app calendar with event reminders, self-hosted Immich server support, cinematic motion effects (Ken Burns, Drift, Vignette Pulse), and OLED burn-in protection. For users who want the frame to be a broader information display — showing weather, playing radio, handling reminders — these extras change what the device does.

Fravora also includes a hidden photos manager (hide specific photos from the slideshow without deleting them, with a thumbnail browser to review and unhide), Smart Shuffle (reduces back-to-back similar shots from bursts), and album-level mixing of different source types in a single rotation.

NAS outage handling

Both apps support NAS, but the reliability features differ. Fravora's SMB implementation includes configurable reconnect intervals (1/5/10/15/30/60 minutes), a retry count setting, non-blocking outage warnings that let the slideshow continue on cached photos, and a blocking overlay only as a last resort when zero images are available from any source. For always-on frames where the NAS occasionally sleeps or reboots, these controls matter for keeping the display running cleanly.

Scheduling depth

One common Fotoo user request visible in reviews is more granular scheduling — per-album or per-hour source switching. Fravora's running hours covers daily on/off schedules with per-day custom windows, plus separate audio running hours. It does not currently support switching albums by time of day, but the schedule-and-standby system is more configurable than a simple on/off timer.

Which should you choose?

Choose Fotoo if: You are familiar with it, already have a license, and its feature set covers what you need. It is a well-established app with a large user base and active development.

Choose Fravora if: You want a free tier to start without paying upfront. You need Immich support. You want voice control, radio, music, or calendar features. You plan to run the app on more than one device. Or you want OLED burn-in protection and cinematic motion effects.
