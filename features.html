Smart digital photo frame features for Android

Fravora focuses on reliable full-screen playback for tablets and dedicated Android display devices. Build a Google Photos slideshow, run an offline NAS photo frame, and add weather, radio, and voice control without subscription pressure.

Offline photo viewer for Android

Use local folders and NAS/SMB shares on your network for private, internet-free playback. Your setup keeps running even when internet drops.

Google Photos slideshow support

Add Google Photos as a cloud source when you want remote access, while still keeping local and NAS sources in the same frame setup.

First-launch onboarding + starter PRO trial

New users get a guided first-run setup flow and a one-time 1-hour PRO trial, so you can configure sources, albums, and optional radio quickly.

Voice-controlled slideshow app

Use wake phrase and command recognition to control slideshow and radio hands-free. Ideal for wall-mounted or hard-to-reach devices.

Smart scheduling

Set daily running hours so your frame wakes and sleeps automatically. Great for kitchens, offices, and living rooms.

Radio with now-playing overlays

Play internet radio while photos run, with station and track information shown in overlay zones you control.

Metadata and weather overlays

Show clock, weather, photo metadata, and now-playing details only where you want, with adjustable size and layout settings.

Cloud and local in one frame

Mix supported cloud providers with local folders and NAS paths so one device can display your full photo library strategy.

SMB outage recovery controls

Set NAS reconnect retry intervals and keep playback running from available photos during network outages, with warning badges that clear after recovery scans.

Smart photo frame alternative without extra hardware

Fravora turns the Android hardware you already own into a dedicated display. Instead of buying a proprietary frame, you can reuse existing tablets, Fire tablets, or Android display devices while keeping your photo files under your control.
