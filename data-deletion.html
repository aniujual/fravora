Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: 07/10/2026

Fravora does not create user accounts. App settings and photo sources are stored locally. If you consent, pseudonymous usage statistics and crash reports are processed by Google Firebase; website analytics is a separate service that runs only with your choice. Fravora contains no advertising.

Even though Fravora does not maintain user accounts, we provide a way for users to request deletion of any data that may be associated with their use of the App, including Firebase data linked to a random app-instance identifier.

What Data Fravora Collects

Fravora may access or process the following types of data:

  • Local device files (photos, images) - processed locally on the device
  • Network information - used to access shared folders or internet radio streams
  • Approximate location - used when you enable weather to request local weather information, and for radio region selection
  • With consent, pseudonymous Firebase Analytics events and Crashlytics reports, linked to a random identifier; Google may process technical data such as an IP address when transmitted

The App does not require the following details for core photo-frame use:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • A Fravora user account
  • A Fravora user profile
  • Registration details
Fravora does not operate its own server for storing photos. Firebase Firestore is used as a temporary relay during TV pairing.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of data associated with your use of Fravora, including pseudonymous analytics or crash-report data processed by Google Firebase, contact us at:

aniujual.dev@yahoo.com

Please include the following information in your request:

  • The name of the app: Fravora
  • The platform you are using (for example, Amazon Appstore)
  • Any additional details that may help us process your request

We will respond to your request within one month. Requests for data held by Google may also need to be made to Google.

What Happens After a Deletion Request

Because Fravora does not store personal accounts or maintain user profiles, deletion requests typically involve:

  • Requesting deletion of Firebase data associated with your app-instance identifier, where available
  • Providing instructions on how to reset or clear data stored locally on your device, including the locally stored consent choice

We will guide you through any steps needed on your device if applicable.
