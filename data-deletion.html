Fravora - Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: 22/03/2026

Fravora does not create user accounts and does not store any personal information on external servers. All data used by the app stays on the user's device unless it is required by third-party services such as advertising networks or analytics providers.

Even though Fravora does not maintain user accounts, we provide a way for users to request deletion of any data that may be associated with their use of the app.

What Data Fravora Collects

Fravora may access or process the following types of data:

  • Local device files (photos, images) - processed locally on the device
  • Network information - used to access shared folders or internet radio streams
  • Approximate location - used only if required by advertising SDKs or radio region selection
  • Advertising identifiers - used by third-party ad networks to show relevant ads

Fravora does not collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Passwords
  • User-generated content
  • Any data that can personally identify a user
Fravora does not store any data on its own servers.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you would like to request deletion of any data associated with your use of Fravora (such as analytics data or advertising identifiers stored by third-party partners), you may contact us at:

aniujual.dev@yahoo.com

Please include the following information in your request:

  • The name of the app: Fravora
  • The platform you are using (for example, Amazon Appstore)
  • Any additional details that may help us process your request

We will respond to your request and complete the deletion process within 30 days.

What Happens After a Deletion Request

Because Fravora does not store personal accounts or maintain user profiles, deletion requests typically involve:

  • Clearing analytics data associated with your device
  • Requesting removal of advertising identifiers from third-party partners
  • Providing instructions on how to reset or clear data stored locally on your device

We will guide you through any steps needed on your device if applicable.
